A town-centre pub with live music every Saturday and a karaoke lounge upstairs. Before, it had a one-page website with a header, a contact form and a phone number, and nothing about opening hours, drinks or who was playing.

  • A look taken from the bar itselfA newspaper front page for the name, the teal of the sign outside, and dark oak like the frames on the walls inside.
  • Their logo, redrawnTraced so it draws itself on when the site opens.
  • The full drinks listFrom the bar’s own menus, with the cocktails and the alcohol-free range.
  • What’s On, every monthEach Saturday’s act from the bar’s poster, updated when the new month’s line-up comes in.
  • Room hire that books itselfThe karaoke lounge at £100 for four hours, with a button that opens the contact form with the booking already picked.
  • On their own web addressMoved onto thefrontpagebar.com without touching the bar’s email, and enquiries go straight to their inbox.
